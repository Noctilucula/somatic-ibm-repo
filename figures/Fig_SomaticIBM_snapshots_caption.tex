\noindent\textbf{Fig.~S1\,|\,Time-resolved compartment maps of
core-gene erosion.} Compartment maps of the mutant (core-gene-loss)
fraction at $t=1{,}200$, $1{,}800$, $2{,}400$, $3{,}600$ and $6{,}000$
nuclear generations for the fragmented (C0), mixed (C2) and strongly
mixed (C3) architectures (model and parameters as in Fig.~2a;
$S=0.004$, $\mu=3\times10^{-4}$; $R=40$ replicates per condition).
For each condition and time point, the replicate whose whole-body
mutant frequency is closest to the ensemble median is shown; the colour
bar gives the mutant fraction per compartment. Under fragmentation,
core-gene loss appears as sharply bounded sectors that expand slowly
and remain distinguishable as separate clones through late times; under
mixing, sector boundaries blur and the loss spreads diffusely through
the tissue.
